# Chat bot

A bot that sits in a Douyin live room as an ordinary viewer's account and talks in the chat, answered by Claude. What it does there:

- **Answers whoever @s it**: questions about the stream, the songs, the streamer, or anything else (with web search). Each @ is free by default, or paid for with the gifts the asker sent the streamer (`--cost 1`, `/修改收费 1`).
- **Joins in without an @** when people talk to or about it (`/自动回复阈值 0.6`; off by default).
- **Answers questions about the room**, such as 「主播会唱晴天吗」 or 「几点开播」, from the room's own files, like its song list and streaming times. Viewers just ask in the chat.
- **Names the songs the streamer sings** (`/识歌`, or every song with `/自动识歌 1`) and says something short about each.
- **Thanks big givers** with a message about their gift (`/自动谢礼 499`), and **works out puzzles on screen** (`/解题`).

To use it:

1. Start, in this order: the speech service (`uv run --directory asr transcribe.py --serve 8758`, from `chat-bot/`), room-feed, logged in as the bot's account, and the bot for a room: `pnpm start --room <房间号> --bot-name <its nickname>` (more under [Start](AGENTS.md#start)). The bot never starts the speech service itself.
2. In the room, an admin (the streamer, or anyone in `config.json`) turns features on and off by @ing the bot with a command. The commands are listed in [`commands.md`](commands.md).
3. To fit it to one room, put that room's details in its `data/<房间号>/` folder, with no code changes: who the streamer is (`context.md`), a song list, commands only that room has, a message for when she sings. How is in [`docs/room-setup.md`](docs/room-setup.md).

## How it works

`chat-bot/` reads a room's chat from room-feed and keeps the last 100 messages in memory. When someone @s the bot's account, it fills [`chat-bot/prompt.md`](prompt.md) with that message, the chat before it and what the streamer said lately, runs `claude -p` (with a screenshot of the live video if `--screenshot` is on; web search and fetch plus read-only access to the chat record (WebSearch, WebFetch, Read, Glob, Grep; `--tools`), no MCP servers, no saved session, since chat is untrusted), and posts `@asker reply` back to the room through room-feed's `POST /api/rooms/:roomId/chat`. Everything the bot says goes out at least 10 seconds apart, while Claude goes on to the next question. Messages wait in four queues, each in order (see "What goes out first" below).

- **What the streamer says**: `chat-bot/asr/transcribe.py` pulls the stream's audio with ffmpeg (the URL comes from room-feed's `GET /api/rooms/:roomId/stream`), finds sentences with fsmn-vad, tells each one's language with FireRedLID, transcribes it with [FireRedASR2-AED](https://github.com/FireRedTeam/FireRedASR2S) or, for Japanese and Korean, SenseVoice Small, and scores each for speech and music with AST (an AudioSet classifier), on Apple's GPU or CUDA. FireRedASR2-AED was picked over SenseVoice Small for making fewer mistakes with a song playing under the speech; it takes about a quarter of a sentence's length to transcribe it on an Apple GPU (SenseVoice took a hundredth), but over its length on the CPU, which then falls behind the stream. It only knows Mandarin, Chinese dialects and English, though: a Japanese song came out as romaji, `<unk>`s or loops like `HELLO HELLO …`, so those lines go to SenseVoice instead. Language ID is unreliable on short sentences, so one under 2 s skips it and goes to FireRedASR2-AED as Mandarin (`lang` empty), and only a sentence FireRedLID is at least 0.8 sure is Japanese or Korean goes to SenseVoice. FireRedLID adds about 0.15–0.2 s a sentence on an Apple GPU, and the log's `heard` shows its `lang` (`zh`, `en`, `ja`, …). FireRedASR2-AED's text has no punctuation (SenseVoice's has), and there's no emotion or background-music tag. Six or more of the same character or phrase (up to 12 characters) in a row are cut down to three. Audio stays in that process and is never written to disk; the bot keeps the text, timestamped, for 30 minutes, and gives the last 5 to the prompt as `{{transcript}}`. The worker is a service of its own (`transcribe.py --serve 8758`, on `127.0.0.1` only, `--asr-port`): you start it by hand, before the bot (see above), and the bot only connects to it, so it outlives the bot. It loads its models once, which takes about half a minute, and the bot waits for that before entering the room, so it hears the streamer from its first minute there; restarting the bot (after a code change, say) connects to the service that's still running and the models stay loaded, with the voiceprints it learned (when the bot disconnects, the service stops listening to the stream and waits for the next connection; stop it for good with Ctrl+C where it runs, or `pkill -f "transcribe.py --serve"`); when the stream ends or its URL expires, the bot asks room-feed again and gives the same worker the new URL (a `{"url": …}` line on the connection), so it's listening again within seconds and still knows the streamer's voice. A song is transcribed as if the streamer said it, so a line with a speech score under 0.2 is marked as sung (see the chat record below); the scores are also in the log (`heard`, as `speech` and `music`). AudioSet labels most songs Music rather than Singing, so the music score counts both.
- **Who's talking**: during a link-up (连线) Douyin mixes both streamers into one audio track, so the worker tells voices apart with CAM++ voiceprints (FunASR's `cam++`, on the CPU). Each sentence joins the closest voice heard so far if it's at least `--same-voice` close (0.6, cosine; set on `transcribe.py`), or starts a new voice; the first voice is the streamer until another has been heard for more than twice as long. Sentences under 1.5 s, sung, or in Japanese or Korean (in these rooms nearly always lyrics, which can score as speech over the music) are left out, as their voiceprints are too rough or not the streamer's talking voice: one sung Japanese line heard first once made the singing "the streamer" and her talking the guest's. Other voices' lines go into the chat record as `[连线]` and into the prompt marked `（连线）`; the log's `heard` shows `speaker`, `host` and `voice` (the similarity) for tuning. A sentence where both talk counts as one voice. The voices are kept for as long as the speech service runs, across bots and rooms alike (it isn't told the room), so restart it when switching rooms, or the last room's streamer stays the streamer.
- **What music is playing**: the worker keeps the stream's last 15 s of audio in memory, and when the bot asks (a `{"music_id": …}` line on the connection, for `/识别背景音乐`) looks it up by fingerprint with Shazam ([shazamio](https://github.com/shazamio/shazamio), an unofficial client: free and keyless, but it may stop working one day). The fingerprint is worked out locally and only it is sent. It finds the recording playing in the background, in any language, usually even with the streamer talking over it; it can't name a song the streamer sings themselves, which `/识歌` tries from the lyrics.
- **Naming the songs sung**: with `--auto-song-id` (or `/自动识歌 1`), once the streamer has been singing a while (in the last minute at least 3 of their lines sung, 70% or more of them) and the song has 5 sung lines, the bot names it as `/识歌` would and says it in the room, only when the model checked the lyrics and is sure; again every 3 minutes of singing on (after 1 minute when it's still the song it named last), never the same song twice in an hour (see [`chat-bot/commands.md`](commands.md#自动识歌)).
- **What's on screen**, while screenshots are on (off by default; `--screenshot` starts with them on, and admins switch them with `/截图 1` and `/截图 0` while it runs; most streams don't need it, lessons with a board do): room-feed's `GET /api/rooms/:roomId/screenshot` (the player only, as a JPEG) goes to Claude with each question, and Claude can zoom into part of it with the `zoom` tool (an MCP server, `chat-bot/src/adapters/zoom-mcp.ts`, cropping with ffmpeg, which must be installed).
- **Long answers**: Claude is asked to answer in one message. When it can't, the answer is split after punctuation into up to `--max-parts` messages, `@昵称 (1/3) …`. The @ uses the whole nickname when the message fits, and its first 3 characters when that's what it takes to fit, or when the answer is split.
- **Chat record**: everything the streamer says, every chat message and every gift goes into `data/<房间号>/chat/<ms>.txt`, one record per line, 100 lines per file, named by its first record's time: `2026-10-16 16:47:22+08:00 [雅#7664182420958889017](gift) 送出 人气票×1（1钻）` (the time in the room's `timeZone`, with its offset; lines without one were written before, in the machine's time zone); a chat's sender also carries what they wear after `|`, as room-feed sends it: `V` or `V年` (直播间会员, monthly or yearly), `灯牌16` (fan club level) and `财富36` (wealth level), as in `[雅#7664182420958889017|V年|灯牌16|财富36](chat)`, or `[主播](speech) …` for the streamer, `[连线](speech) …` for whoever they're linked up with, `[主播](sing) …` when sung (a speech score under 0.2; the prompt's transcript marks those lines `（唱）`). Lines rather than JSON halve what Claude spends reading them. Claude gets the folder (`--add-dir`) and is told in the prompt (`{{chatDir}}`) that it's the room's chat record, to read when the recent lines in the prompt aren't enough. Its file tools aren't pre-approved, so they reach only that folder: Claude Code denies reads anywhere else when nobody is there to approve them. The record is kept across runs.
- **Paying with gifts**: every gift a viewer sends in the room adds its diamonds (抖币) to their balance, and each @mention costs `--cost` diamonds (0 by default, so nobody pays and none of this applies). A viewer who can't pay gets `@昵称 @我要先给主播送礼：每次1钻，你送的还剩0钻（不另外扣钱），送了马上回你` instead of an answer, at most once a minute (and when more than 3 notices come in a minute, the waiting ones are dropped, the room gets one `想让我回你的，要先给主播送礼…` without an @, and there are no notices for 5 minutes), and their mention (the last one) waits: when they send a gift within 5 minutes, it's paid for and answered at once (a gift still too small keeps it waiting another 5 minutes); a mention that fails is refunded. Balances outlast a restart: they're written to `data/<房间号>/wallet.json` (`savedAt` and, per user id, `gifted` and `spent`) as they change and when the bot exits, and cleared when it starts only if they were last saved more than 24 hours ago (or the file has no `savedAt`). Gift events need room-feed logged in.
- **Admins and commands**: `chat-bot/config.json` lists the admins' user ids (`"admins": ["7664182420958889017"]`), who they are to viewers (`"adminNames": "雅"`, for 「只有主播和雅才可以」). Who the bot plays is the room's own `data/<房间号>/persona.md` (`{{persona}}` turns it into `你的人设是…`; empty for none), or a shared persona in `data/personas/<名字>.md` that the room builds on, with its `persona.md` changing only the sections it names (`"persona": { "base": "fairy", "vars": { "主播": "茜茜" } }` in `room_config.json`; see [docs/room-setup.md](docs/room-setup.md)). All are read on every use, so an edit applies at once; the bot never writes them. The bot reads the admins again whenever the file changes, so they can be added or removed while it runs; an edit that doesn't parse keeps the last good list (and logs a warning). The room's streamer is an admin too: room-feed reads their user id from the room page (`GET /api/rooms/:roomId`, `meta.uid`), and the bot asks for it every 10 seconds until it's there (`streamer is an admin` in the log), then writes the streamer's nickname and user id into the room's `data/<房间号>/room_config.json` (`"streamer": { "nickname", "uid" }`). A mention with a `/` first runs a command, like `@机器人/修改人设 爱讲冷笑话的小猫`: admin-only ones can also be asked for in words (「把你的人设改成小猫」: Claude names the command, and the bot runs it for an admin and refuses anyone else); see [`chat-bot/commands.md`](commands.md) for each command, who may run it, what it costs, how they're handled and how to add one. In a private room (私密直播间) Douyin hides every viewer's user id, admins' and probably the streamer's included, so admins aren't recognised there: to run a command, add `"111111"` to the admins (every viewer there becomes one) and take it out again after.
- **Talking unprompted**: when someone talks to or about the bot without @ing it, it joins in by answering them like an @ from that person (with `prompt.md`, charged like a mention). Every 20 seconds it asks [Jev](https://typesafe.ai) (a fast yes/no classifier; put `JEV_API_KEY` in `chat-bot/.env`) how likely each new chat and thing the streamer said is about the bot; at `--auto-reply-threshold` or more (`1` by default, so off; `/自动回复阈值 0.6` turns it on), or whatever someone the bot answered in the last minute says (it's likely a reply to it), Codex picks, from [`chat-bot/reply-pick.md`](reply-pick.md), whom to answer (the lines that still need it, at most 3, maybe none), and each is answered like an @ from that person (charged, with their own past exchanges; the streamer's spoken lines as the streamer's, by their uid, free). Without Codex (`--codex ''`) it doesn't talk unprompted at all. It keeps quiet for at least `--auto-reply-interval` seconds (60) after talking; `/自动回复间隔 0` doesn't wait. Streamers often read the chat aloud: what they read is matched to the chat by sound (Smith-Waterman alignment of the pinyin, `src/core/reading.ts`) and marked `（念了某人的弹幕）`, so it isn't taken for them saying it. Admins change the threshold with `/自动回复阈值 0.7` (`1` turns it off).
- **Thanking big givers**: when a viewer's gifts within 5 minutes come to more than `--thanks-threshold` diamonds (99999 by default, so off; admins change it with `/自动谢礼 100`), Claude reads that viewer's own recent messages, their past exchanges with the bot, the room's chat and `context.md`, and writes one @ message to thank them from `gift-thanks.md`. The prompt keeps it conservative: no comments on looks, age, money or how much they gave, no guesses about who they are, no jokes or teasing, no comparing viewers. It waits 15 seconds first, so a combo is thanked once, for everything in it. What a viewer sends after a thanks counts afresh, and is thanked again once it passes the threshold too.
- **Prompts and room files**: the prompts are shared by every room: [`chat-bot/song-id.md`](song-id.md) for `/识歌`, [`chat-bot/solve.md`](solve.md) for `/解题`, [`chat-bot/gift-thanks.md`](gift-thanks.md) for thanking big givers and [`chat-bot/song-intro.md`](song-intro.md) for what the bot says about a song once it's named, besides `prompt.md`. [`chat-bot/intro.md`](intro.md) is what the bot says about itself (who made it, what it can and can't do), as `{{intro}}` in `prompt.md`, so it answers questions about itself the same way every time. [`chat-bot/limitations.md`](limitations.md) is what it can't do and why (mishearing, link-up voices, not seeing the video, why it sometimes doesn't answer, memory, song ID, chat limits), as `{{limitations}}` in `prompt.md`, so when viewers notice something wrong it can explain in a line; it's read on every use. What's particular to a room goes in `data/<房间号>/context.md` (who the streamer is, running jokes, what to mind), which every prompt gets as `{{context}}`; the bot creates it empty when missing. A room can still override a prompt by putting a file of the same name there with `_` for `-`: its own `song_id.md` overrides `song-id.md` (`prompt.md` has no room override). All of these are read every time they're used, so edits apply while the bot runs.
- **Seeing gifts**: every prompt's `{{history}}` shows the gifts of the last 5 minutes among the chat, by time (`澳***: （送出 比心兔兔×1，299钻）`), so when the streamer says 「谢一下这个礼物」 the bot knows which one.
- **Setting up a room**: each room's folder can hold its own commands (「主播会唱xxx吗」, answered from its song list), files to download on entering, a message for when the streamer sings, and its own prompts; how is in [`chat-bot/docs/room-setup.md`](docs/room-setup.md).
- **Knowing its own settings and commands**: `prompt.md` gets the room's settings as they are now (`{{settings}}`: what an @ costs, screenshots on or off, the auto-reply threshold, the gift-thanks threshold, and for a mention the asker's balance) and the command list from [`chat-bot/commands.md`](commands.md) (`{{commands}}`: its "The commands" section, read on every use). So a viewer asking what song this is can be pointed to `/识歌`, one asking why nobody thanked them told that thanks are an admin setting, and a streamer asking why the bot is quiet told how @s and auto-reply work.
- **Not repeating itself**: everything the bot sends goes into the room's `bot_logs.md`, and each @ and its answer into `<user id>/talk.txt`. Every prompt gets the bot's last 40 messages (`{{botTalk}}`), and a mention also the asker's last 10 exchanges (`{{userHistory}}`), with the instruction not to say the same things again. These are plain text rather than resumed Claude Code sessions, which would replay every earlier prompt (transcripts, chat, screenshots) and grow without end.
- **What goes out first**: the bot sends one message at a time (`--send-interval`), from four queues: replies, then system messages (command results), then low-balance notices, then what it says unprompted.

It needs [room-feed](../room-feed) running for the room and logged in as the bot's account (`pnpm room-feed login`), Claude Code's `claude`, `ffmpeg` and [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/) on `PATH`. The first run downloads the speech models: FireRedASR2-AED (4.7 GB) and FireRedLID (3.5 GB) into `~/.cache/fireredasr2`, fsmn-vad and SenseVoice Small (0.9 GB) into `~/.cache/modelscope` and AST into `~/.cache/huggingface`. From `chat-bot/`, start the speech service first, and leave it running (it keeps the models loaded, so restarting the bot after a code change doesn't reload them):

```sh
uv run --directory asr transcribe.py --serve 8758
```

then room-feed (from the repo root: `pnpm room-feed --room <房间号>`), then the bot:

```sh
pnpm start --room <房间号> --bot-name <the logged-in account's nickname> [--bot-id <its uid>]
```

Without the speech service running, the bot still enters the room, without speech (it logs `no speech service on port 8758: start it first, …` once), and starts hearing the streamer within 10 s of it being started; `--no-asr` turns speech off altogether.

Options that are a room's settings (`--cost`, `--screenshot`, `--auto-reply-threshold`, `--auto-reply-interval`, `--thanks-threshold`, `--auto-song-id`, `--malice-watch`) only seed them: the first time a room is started, a setting its `data/<房间号>/room_config.json` doesn't have yet is filled in with the option you gave, else its default; after that the file is the source of truth, read again whenever it changes, and the admin commands write to it (fields and defaults in [`docs/room-setup.md`](docs/room-setup.md)). An option that differs from what the file has is ignored, with a `setting from room_config, ignoring option` warning.

The console shows info and up (mentions, gifts, what was heard, replies); `logs/chat-bot.log` has everything, including every chat line and prompt, as JSON lines. `--bot-id` makes @mentions match by user id rather than nickname; room-feed shows it as the `user.id` of the bot's own chat messages.

The room-feed client (`RoomFeedClient`), its event types and the port and room-number checks come from [`@dy-apps/room-feed-protocol`](../room-feed-protocol), a workspace package; the bot imports nothing from the site.
